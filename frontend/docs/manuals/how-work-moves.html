How work moves at Following

Step by step, for every kind of campaign: who does it, and where in the platform. Every address is on platform.following.ae.

The one habit that makes it work Start every day on Today (/work/today). It lists what is waiting on you, oldest first, and every row opens the screen where you do it. If it is not on Today, it is not yours yet.

Who does what

  • Business development brings brands in and logs every deal.
  • Founders (CEO and co-founder) release a brand to the talent team, set sell prices, clear creators, approve money and send things out.
  • Talent find creators, agree their rates, book them and chase their content.
  • Account managers own the client: proposals out, picks back, content reviews, reports.

1A brand comes in (every campaign starts here)

  1. Business development logs the brand and the deal in Sales (/work/sales). Log it on day one, even if it is only interest.
  2. A founder releases it to the talent team. Either Start sourcing on Rosters (/work/areas), or Ask talent on the deal in Sales. Both are founders only. Ask talent reuses the brand's roster if it already has one.
  3. The founder writes the brief: market, categories, follower range, what we need back, paid or barter, and the budget per creator as a range (for example AED 3,500 to 4,000). The brief shows any part that is missing.
  4. The talent team is alerted at once, and the brand's roster opens. One roster per brand, for its whole life.

2Sourcing creators (managed paid and barter)

Everything happens on the brand's roster. It replaces the campaign Google Sheet.

Talent

  1. Open the brand's roster from Today or Rosters.
  2. Search the master database first (Add creators). We hold hundreds of creators with prices on them, including everything from the old master sheet.
  3. For anyone new, use Paste a list: rows straight from a sheet, or one handle per line. Check what it read, then add. New creators go to the waiting room without running analytics.
  4. Every row says its next step and carries one button for it: Add rate, Send for review, Add to proposal, Book. Skip and a … menu sit beside it.
  5. Click Add rate on a row and record both numbers: what they asked and what you agreed, in their currency, and what it buys (reel, reel also on TikTok, full scope and so on). The AED is worked out for you.
  6. Before agreeing, read Last time: what they agreed with us on another campaign. A price above the brief's budget, or well above last time, turns amber.
  7. Tick the good ones and press Send for review. That is the message "these are good, please add sell prices". The founders see it on Today.
  8. For the rest, press Skip (not for this campaign) and give a reason if you have one. They stay in the database, and the reason follows them to the next brief.
  9. Click a creator's name to see everything we know: every price they gave us, campaigns with us, past reasons, and tags like "Reliable". Add their WhatsApp or email there; only the founders can read it back.

Founders

  1. Open the roster from Today ("Price N shortlisted creators").
  2. Press Price and clear. Enter each sell price; the margin shows as you type. They are made sellable and cleared in one step.
  3. For the ones that miss, tick them and press Send back with a note ("too expensive, find options at 3.5k"). The note shows on the row and on talent's Today.

2BOpen call on WhatsApp New

A campaign card on a roster that creators answer on WhatsApp. Our agent on the marketing number collects their price, dates and email, and puts them on the roster for you.

  1. A superadmin creates it on the roster: Create an open call. Describe the brand, never name it. The target per creator and the ceiling are for the agent only and are never shown to a creator. Then Make it live.
  2. Talent send it out. Copy a DM with my link (or Copy my link) and send it to creators on Instagram. Every creator who answers through your link is credited to you.
  3. The agent does the asking. It reads the creator's price and dates and offers one counter if they ask above target. Whoever answers lands on the roster with their quote, ready for Send for review.
  4. Yours now means the agent stepped back: the price is above the ceiling, or the creator is big enough that a person should talk to them. Open Conversations, reply yourself, then Hand back to the agent when you are done.
  5. Superadmins can also Copy the story link, Invite app creators (a push to the creator app) and Invite on WhatsApp.
  6. When the client has chosen, a founder presses Tell creators how it went, so nobody is left waiting.

3The proposal (managed paid and barter)

  1. On the roster, press Add to a proposal and pick the proposal. Before you press add it shows who cannot go on and why: not cleared, no sell price, the client already said no, or already on it.
  2. Check the proposal in Proposals. When it is ready, a founder marks it internally approved.
  3. Existing client: a founder marks the link ready to send. It appears in To send (/work/handovers). The account manager sends it from their own inbox and marks it sent. The platform never emails a client by itself.
  4. New client: generate the share link. They see five sample creators; the rest unlock once the agreement is signed and the advance is paid, both marked by a founder.
  5. The client picks inside the proposal, or tells you by email, WhatsApp or a call and a founder uses Confirm for the client.
  6. Confirming locks the creators they took. If they want more, Reopen: they choose again, and the ones already confirmed stay confirmed.

4AManaged paid campaign

  1. Settle the real costs. The proposal price is a quote. Founders confirm what each creator is actually paid on Rates (/work/negotiations).
  2. Open the campaign once the client's budget is spent or close to it. A later round joins the same campaign.
  3. Each creator moves along the Delivery tab of the campaign. Every row says the next thing to do:
    • Enrolled: agree a rate (talent)
    • Rate agreed: get the agreement signed (the superadmin creates the enrolment link; talent is told on the bell and by email and sends it to the creator on WhatsApp; the creator signs and gives bank details on it)
    • Agreement on file: send the brief / shooting guide (talent)
    • Guide sent: chase the content (talent)
    • Content in: review the content (account manager). With Frame.io, press Send for review; the client approves or asks for changes inside our platform
    • Content approved: get the post link (talent). Paste it on the campaign's Performance tab; a founder releases the results to the client
    • Posted: pay the creator (founders, on Payouts)
  4. Pipeline (/work/pipeline) shows every creator on every campaign with that next step, so nobody works from memory.
  5. Invoice the client from the campaign's Money tab (founders only).

4BManaged barter campaign

  1. Sourcing and the proposal are the same as Parts 2 and 3. A barter proposal needs no sell price.
  2. Talent ask each creator; a creator stays "asking" until they agree. Only creators who agreed reach the client.
  3. Open the campaign. Track the product on the creator: product ready, dispatched, received. For a visit, the venue scans the creator's QR code and enters their 6-digit code.
  4. If the barter copy promises the creator a follow-up ("our team will message you with the branch"), write the handover note on the campaign. Every approved creator then becomes a task on talent's Today, so the promise has an owner.
  5. Content, post link and results work exactly as in paid. There is no payout.

5App campaigns (creators apply in the Following app)

Three types: barter, paid deal (paid from the client's funding pool), and cashback (a receipt from the venue).

  1. Create the campaign. If the client has no platform login, tick "No brand account, we manage this".
  2. Share the application link. Creators apply in the app, with their details already on file.
  3. The brand approves every creator (or our team does, for a campaign we manage). An application nobody answers is declined after 30 days, never let in by silence.
  4. Approved creators see their deliverables in the app and submit proof there. We verify it.
  5. Cashback: the creator uploads the receipt and a founder approves the claim, which credits their wallet.
  6. Paid deal: paid from the client's funding pool once the work is verified.

6Creator contracting (the client finds the creator, we contract and pay)

  1. The brand creates the order in their account: one creator (New payment) or many on one invoice (Pay several).
  2. We raise the invoice. It shows on our Today.
  3. When the money lands, a founder marks it in after checking the bank. That sends each creator their link.
  4. The creator signs the agreement with us and confirms their bank details on the link.
  5. We pay the creator. The brand sees each step on their own screen and can download the signed agreement.

7Following Track: links, clicks and sales New

One short link per creator per retailer. We count every tap, and with the store pixel we see the journey through to the purchase: product views, carts, checkouts and orders, per creator.

  1. Open the campaign, then the Links tab. Affiliate links in the sidebar lists every campaign that has links.
  2. Retailers: add where the links should send people (the brand's site, or a retailer such as a beauty chain). Adding one makes a link for everyone on the roster.
  3. Creators' links: press Copy all links and give each creator their own. Use A link for someone not on the roster for a brand ambassador; their clicks and sales count too.
  4. If the brand gives its own discount codes, enter them under Brand's codes so sales on a code are credited to the right creator.
  5. Store pixel: send the brand the Shopify custom pixel or the Pixel for any website. Once it is on their store, carts, checkouts and orders arrive by themselves.
  6. Sales can also come from the retailer's own report: Add one, or Import a spreadsheet. A retailer with a postback sends them automatically (Postback for the retailer).
  7. Nothing reaches the brand until a superadmin turns on Show the brand. The brand then sees Following Track on their campaign: clicks, sales and the journey, per creator.

8Money: who may do what

  • Talent record what a creator charges us. They never see sell prices or margins.
  • Account managers see sell prices, never our cost.
  • Only founders set sell prices, clear creators, confirm rates, approve and mark payments, and see the Money tab.
  • Exports are founders only. Never send a client a spreadsheet; send the proposal or share link.

What is still not covered

So nobody is surprised:

  • WhatsApp messages that start a conversation (Invite on WhatsApp, Tell creators how it went) only go out once Meta has approved them. Replies inside a chat the creator started go out at any time within 24 hours.
  • Opening a campaign before the budget is spent is not blocked. It relies on the founder's judgement.
  • Managed campaigns do not appear in the creator app. Open calls do, through Invite app creators.
  • 435 of the 751 creators in the database have no country set, so they appear in no market pack until someone sets it.
  • The old master sheet still sits in a personal Google account. Move it to a company account, then stop using it.
