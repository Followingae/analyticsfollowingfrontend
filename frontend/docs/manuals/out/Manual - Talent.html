01 · Your day

How a day goes

Three moments. Everything else finds you.

First thing
  • Open Requests. Everything anybody is waiting on from you.
  • Open Today. The rest of what is yours.
  • Open Pipeline. Every creator we owe something.
Through the day
  • Work from Pipeline, not from memory or WhatsApp.
  • Each row tells you the verb. Do that one thing.
  • If it only happened in chat, nobody else can see it.
Before you log off
  • Nothing red in Pipeline without a reason written down.
  • Move it, note it, or nudge whoever is blocking it.

05 · Your jobs

What you actually do

In order. The strip on each card is what to click, as you click it.

1

Find creators for a brand

Starts when a brief arrives. Ends when the client has a list to choose from.
Go toCreatorsRosters
  1. The Founder & CEO writes the brief and releases the brand. It reaches you on Today.
  2. The brand’s roster is already open. It replaces the campaign sheet.
  3. Search the master database first with Add creators.
  4. Anyone new: Paste a list, rows from a sheet or one handle per line.
  5. ScoutOutreach adds creators to the same roster. Its rows say so in the source tabs.
  6. Nobody tells you how many to find. A client signing fifteen may need to see hundreds.
Next: You price each one on the same roster, then send the good ones for review.
2

Price them and send them up

As you find them. A creator with no price cannot be sold.
Go toCreatorsRostersOpen the brand’s roster
  1. Each row says its next step and has one button. Needs a rate means Add rate.
  2. Record what they asked and what you agreed, in their currency, and what it buys.
  3. Read Last time first. Amber means over budget or well above last time.
  4. Rate in, send for review means press Send for review. Tick several to send at once.
  5. The rest: Skip, with a reason if you have one. They stay in the database.
  6. Everything else on a row is in its … menu.
Next: A founder presses Price on each. Priced creators come back to the roster as Ready.
3

Work an open call

When a superadmin has put an open call live on the roster.
Go toCreatorsRostersOpen the brand’s roster
  1. Press Copy a DM with my link and send it to creators on Instagram.
  2. ScoutAI, on WhatsApp, explains the brief and asks their rate for you.
  3. Whoever answers lands on the roster with a quote, credited to you.
  4. Yours now means a person should talk to them. Open Conversations.
  5. Reply, then press Hand back to the agent when you are done.
Next: They go up for review like any creator you priced yourself.
4

Work beside ScoutOutreach

Every day a roster has outreach running. Its chapter follows these jobs.
Go toScoutOutreach
  1. Keep sourcing your own creators. It adds to your roster, it does not replace you.
  2. Glance at the live bar: DMs sent today and the job it is on now.
  3. Rows that say ScoutOutreach or ScoutAI is getting the rate need nothing from you.
  4. Say yes or no on what it finds. That teaches its screening for the campaign.
  5. Stopped and needs a person: sign in on the bot PC, then press Resume.
Next: Rates land on the roster by themselves. You send them for review.
5

Send the enrolment link

When the bell and an email say "Send @creator their enrolment link".
Go toCampaignsEnrolments
  1. A founder created it. The link is inside the bell and the email.
  2. Send it to the creator on WhatsApp the same day. It is personal.
  3. They fill in their details, sign, upload their passport and give their bank.
  4. Never type a creator’s bank details for them.
  5. Chase anyone who has not finished. The screen shows who is where.
Next: Signed creators move to Payables. A founder releases the money.
6

Get the content in

From the agreed date until the post is live.
Go toCampaignsChasing
  1. This screen is who is late, who is due, who has gone quiet.
  2. Collect the content and the creator’s own insights screenshots.
  3. Put the posted link on the creator’s row when it goes live.
Next: Account management reports it to the client.
7

Give each creator their link

On a campaign that sells, once its retailers are set up.
Go toCampaignsOpen a campaignLinks
  1. Every creator on the roster has their own short link per retailer.
  2. Press Copy all links and send each creator only their own.
  3. Their clicks, carts and sales are counted against that link.
Next: A superadmin decides when the brand sees the numbers.

ScoutOutreach and ScoutAI, continued

How each one works

ScoutOutreach finds them
  • One roster, one brief: location, follower range, language.
  • Lives at ScoutOutreach, with a live bar. Each roster shows its own strip too.
  • Screens on follower range, a post in 60 days, a real creator, not based elsewhere. Then an AI reads each against the brief and the team’s own yes and no, with a written reason.
  • A hashtag search reads the 40 most recent posts from up to 4 hashtags. Light analytics on each creator costs about $0.008.
  • The DM bot sends from the company’s Instagram, in Chrome on an office PC. No link: "Interested? Just reply here". ScoutAI holds the chat.
  • Creators who list an email get one automatically from hello@following.ae, with a WhatsApp button.
  • WhatsApp alerts go to creators in our network on Meta-approved templates. Paid is never called barter.
ScoutAI talks to them
  • A creator who replies to the DM talks to ScoutAI in that same Instagram chat, in English.
  • On WhatsApp, a campaign can run an open call with a code, like OC-D7F4E.
  • It explains the brief and asks the screening questions set for the campaign.
  • For paid work it asks their rate, and can counter within that campaign’s negotiation rules.
  • Creators answer with tap buttons or in their own words.
  • It adds them to the roster with their rate. A decline or a poor fit is recorded with the reason.
  • Its WhatsApp chats are kept on the Desk and never ring the team’s bell.

Enrolments, start to finish, continued

What the creator does

1Email
Verifies their email with a code.
2Details
Name, contact and address. New links ask Home or Office. A home is a villa or an apartment: number, floor, building or community. An office has the company name.
3Agreement
Reads and signs it.
4Passport
Uploads it. The system reads the name and the expiry.
5Bank
Gives an IBAN, or for an exchange-house payout, the city they collect in.
The payee confirms itself when
  • The bank account is in the creator’s own signed name. A shortened surname still counts.
  • A passport is uploaded.
  • It is not a company account.
  • Anything else comes to a person, with the reason shown.
Passports
  • The system checks the name and the expiry date.
  • A mismatch comes to a person, on the enrolment record.
  • Looks right accepts it. Send back reopens the creator’s passport step.
  • After a send back, talent tells the creator. They will not know otherwise.
Your part
Go toCampaignsEnrolments
  1. The bell and an email say Send @creator their enrolment link. The link is inside.
  2. Send it on WhatsApp the same day. Never post a link anywhere public.
  3. Chase anyone who has not finished. Campaigns, then Enrolments, shows who is where.
  4. Answer their questions about the form. Money questions go to a founder.

09 · Right and wrong

What good looks like

Everything on the right has actually happened here and cost us something.

What good looks like
  • Everything you find goes on the roster, the same hour you find it.
  • A price is recorded as two numbers: what they ask, what they agreed.
  • A creator you cannot place in a country is still worth adding; say so in the note.
  • When a brief is missing market, category or budget, you ask before you start.
  • An enrolment link reaches the creator the same day the bell tells you.
  • You take a request the moment you start it, so nobody duplicates your work.
What has actually gone wrong
  • Sourcing into a spreadsheet and uploading at the end. Nobody can see progress, and the half you have done looks like nothing. The roster is the sheet.
  • Recording only the published rate. We then sell at a margin that does not exist.
  • Guessing a country code on a phone number. We once sent a Saudi creator’s signing link to a stranger in the UAE.
  • Agreeing a fee in WhatsApp and typing it in later. The number drifts, every time.
  • Leaving a request unclaimed while you work on it. Somebody else starts it too.
10 · Vocabulary

Words we use

They mean something specific. A roster is not a list, and cost is not sell.

Brand
A company we are talking to. Becomes a client when a deal is won.
Client
A brand that has bought something. They have an account and can log in.
Deal
One conversation about money. A brand can have many, over years.
Campaign
A deal that was won and opened. Real creators, real money.
Roster
The creators sourced for one brand, for its whole life. It replaced the sheet.
Skip
Not for this campaign. Still in the database; the reason follows them.
Open call
A campaign card creators answer on WhatsApp. The agent collects their price.
ScoutOutreach
Finds creators for a brief, screens them, and DMs or emails them.
ScoutAI
Talks to creators on Instagram or WhatsApp and puts their rate on the roster.
Following Track
Short links per creator. Clicks, carts and sales, shown to the brand once released.
List
A standing question: "fashion, UAE". Answers itself. Never client-specific.
Cost
What we pay a creator. Founders and talent only. Never shown to a brand.
Sell
What we charge a client. A creator with no sell price cannot be sold.
Quoted cost
What we assumed when pricing. Rarely what we end up paying.
Agreed rate
What a creator actually accepted. The only number that is real.
Request
Something somebody is waiting on, with an owner and a date.
Proposal
A priced roster the Founder & CEO sends a client to choose from.
Share link
A /p/ link a client opens without logging in. Only the Founder & CEO sends one.
Enrolment link
A founder creates it, talent sends it, the creator signs and gives their own bank.
The ladder
The rungs a booked creator climbs: briefed, content in, posted, paid.
Held
Stopped until a founder answers. Nothing has happened yet.
11 · The hard rules

Rules that cost money

Each one has already cost us something.

  • A creator with no price cannot be sold. Record it on the roster or they do not exist to us.
  • Skip is not a no. Give the reason; it follows them to the next brief.
  • An enrolment link is personal. Never post one in a group or anywhere public.
  • Never guess a creator’s country code. We once sent a Saudi creator’s signing link to a stranger.
  • The agreed rate is the one we use, not their published rate.
  • A creator signs their own paperwork and enters their own bank details.
  • Nothing is real until it is on the screen. WhatsApp is not a record.

Every screen in the platform is explained under Guide in the menu. If something here does not match what you see, the product is right and this is out of date. Say so and it gets reprinted.
