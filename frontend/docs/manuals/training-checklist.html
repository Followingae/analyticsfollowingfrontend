Team training checklist

Platform launch to the team
Karachi office, 16 November 2026

0Before anyone sits down

  • TV on, showing the wall (Karachi HQ paired to Office wall - main). Sound on.
  • Every manual printed or on each laptop. Guide in the menu opens for each login.
  • Logins work: Sana, Aisha, Hajar, Aqsa, Ushna (first login, change password).
  • Your own screen on Today, zoomed for the room.

1Open the session everyone · 15 min

  • Why: the platform is the record. Sheets, email and WhatsApp are no longer where work lives.
  • Walk the How work moves page: brand in, sourcing, proposal, delivery, money, the wall.
  • The wall: it shows what you record, a second later. Never money. Nothing by hand.
  • Do one live move on your screen and let the room watch it reach the TV.
  • Vocabulary: brand, client, deal, campaign, roster, request, owner, rung, held.
Say out loud
  • If it is not on the screen it did not happen.
  • A request with your name on it is yours until it is done.
  • Cost is never visible to a brand. Only founders and talent see it.
  • No automated email ever reaches a client. A person presses send.
  • Only the Founder & CEO sends a proposal, quote or share link.

2Everyone, on their own login 10 min

  • Today: the four numbers, then what is waiting on you. Oldest first.
  • Waiting on me: anything held for your decision.
  • Requests: raise one, take one, mark one done. Due date on every one.
  • Guide in the menu: their own role at the top, the other roles behind it.
  • The menu is role-shaped. Show that a talent login and an account login differ.

3Talent Hajar, Aqsa, Ushna · 25 min

  • Pipeline: every creator we owe something, with the verb. Work from it, not memory.
  • A brief reaches Today; the brand's roster is the sheet. Add creators, Paste a list.
  • Add rate: what they asked and what they agreed, two numbers. Last time, amber.
  • Send for review, Skip with a reason. Priced creators come back as Ready.
  • Requests that say Message @creator: message them yourself, mark done. Six are open now.
  • Enrolment link: the bell and email carry it; send on WhatsApp the same day. Never public.
  • Chasing: who is late, due, quiet. Post link goes on the row.
  • Payables: every amount we owe a creator. Cash on barter is internal. Leadership releases.
  • ScoutOutreach board and ScoutAI: yes or no on what it finds; Resume when it stops.
  • Hajar: give each campaign to a talent manager on Pipeline. Do it live for Jollibee and Barakat.

4Account management Sana · 20 min

  • Clients: your name on the client record is what fills your Today. Barakat, BFL, Jollibee, BlackOut.
  • A client asks on WhatsApp: answer there, then raise it as a Request ticked "a client asked".
  • Campaign shell, five tabs: Story, Delivery, Content, Performance, Links.
  • Delivery: move the rung the day it happens. The status sheet is retired.
  • Content: Send for review; the client approves inside the platform.
  • Performance: paste the live post link. Leadership releases it to the client.
  • Hand her the open items: who posted on BFL Oman, Bahrain, Kuwait; two unplaced payables.

5Business development Aisha · 15 min

  • Sales: the board is the record. Every deal carries a next step and a date.
  • Partner leads: website and terminal enquiries land here; terminal leads reach RFM by themselves.
  • The moment a deal looks real: get the brand's brief, forward it to the Founder & CEO. Never set it up.
  • Quotes and proposals go from the Founder & CEO only. Never a spreadsheet.
  • Hand over in writing on the brand. Merge the two Nestle deals if one contact.

6Leadership Hajar with you · 15 min

  • Rates: clients who said yes, waiting for cost. Price on the row; margin as you type.
  • Clear a roster: Price or Send back with a reason. Strike with a reason, always.
  • Approvals and Payables: release money; the funded position informs, never blocks.
  • Enrolments: passports that did not match, payees that did not confirm themselves.
  • Office displays: what each screen shows; only leadership pairs a money display.

7Close everyone · 10 min

  • Each person does one real action on their own login and sees it on the wall.
  • 09:30 daily: account management and talent run the open items from Today and Requests, not a chat.
  • Anything decided in WhatsApp today gets raised as a request before leaving.
  • Where to say "the manual is wrong": tell you, it gets reprinted.
  • Still outside the platform, say so plainly: on-call rota, reply-time clocks, monthly calendar, escalation ladder.

8Do not say in the room

  • Cash paid to Jollibee creators is agency-side. Never in front of a client, never on the wall.
  • Self-serve is parked. Do not demo paying or signing up as a brand.
  • Pizza Hut is the demo client. Do not touch it or assign it.
