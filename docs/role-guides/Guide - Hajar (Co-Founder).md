# Co-Founder — your guide

**Who this is for:** Hajar. Full access to everything, and the final word on creator quality,
creator pricing, and money going out.

**Your one sentence:** nothing weak goes to a client, nothing overpriced gets agreed, and
nothing leaves the bank without you or Zain saying yes.

You have the same access as Zain. This guide covers the parts of the job that are actually
yours day to day, not the whole platform.

---

## 1. Your day, in order

**First thing in the morning**

1. **Today** (`/work/today`). Your home screen, built for leadership. Four numbers at the top,
   then what is waiting on you and what is running without you.
2. **Decisions** (`/work/inbox`). Every approval, anywhere in the platform, in one list with the
   oldest first. This is the screen that replaces people chasing you on WhatsApp.
3. **Pipeline** (`/work/pipeline`). Every creator across every brand, and the next action on
   each. Red means late. The column on the left is the whole process with counts, so you can
   read the state of the company without opening anything.

**Weekly**

- **Targets** (`/work/goals`) and **Coverage** (`/work/coverage`). Where we are against what we
  said, and which clients have nothing moving.
- **Payout runs** (`/work/payouts`). What is about to be paid, before it is paid.

## 2. Approving creator lists (your biggest lever)

Every list of creators passes you before a client sees it. You are the reason a client never
receives a bad list.

**Where:** the brand's **Roster** (`/work/areas`), or the list on the quote
(`/work/proposals/[id]`).

**What you are checking, in order**

1. **Budget.** Is every creator inside the brief's budget per creator? A creator who charges
   20,000 on a 5,000 brief does not belong on the list, however good they are.
2. **Market.** Is the creator actually in the market the brief asked for? Check where they
   really live, not what the bio says. This has gone wrong before.
3. **Quality.** Engagement against follower count. A large account with weak engagement is
   worse than a small one with strong engagement.
4. **Repetition.** Has this brand used this creator recently? Has this creator appeared on three
   other campaigns this month? Clients notice, and it is the fastest way to look lazy.
5. **Fit.** Does the creator's content actually look like the brand?
6. **Depth.** Are there enough options per market, or is one market carrying three names?

**The steps**

1. Go through the list and remove what fails. Say which one failed and why, in one line each.
   "Remove @handle, based in Florida not UAE" teaches. "Not suitable" does not.
2. Where a rate is too high, **say the number you will pay**, not "too expensive". "Offer max
   1,000" ends the conversation in one message.
3. If the list is thin, say how many more and by when. A review without a deadline gets read as
   optional.
4. Approve what passes. That is what releases it to the client.

## 3. Approving money

Only you and Zain can release money. Everything is prepared by the team and waits for you.

**The steps**

1. **Payables** (`/work/payables`). Every amount we owe a creator, with the creator, the
   campaign, the amount, and whether we hold their bank details.
2. Check three things before you approve: the amount matches what was agreed, the work was
   actually delivered, and we hold bank details. The screen tells you the third.
3. Approve. Above AED 25,000 the platform asks you to type the amount to confirm. That is
   deliberate, not a glitch.
4. **Payout runs** (`/work/payouts`). Approved payables are batched into a run, which produces
   the bank file. Open the run and read it before you act on it. A line with no bank details
   cannot go into a run and the screen says so per line.
5. After paying, the receipts go to the creators. Confirm that happened.

**What to refuse**

- An amount that does not match the agreed rate, with no explanation.
- A payment for content that was never posted, or was posted and then deleted.
- A creator we have no bank details for. Approving it just moves the problem.

**Answering from email.** When the team asks you for a money decision you get an email with the
details and two buttons. You can decide from the email without logging in. The link is good for
seven days and works once. If somebody nudges you, you get a fresh link and the old one stops
working.

## 4. Holding the team to output

This is the part that is hardest to do from a screen, and the platform now makes it visible.

- **Pipeline** shows anything late in red, and who owns it. A row with no owner is the real
  problem, not the delay. Tick the rows and use "Give to".
- **Chasing** (`/work/chasing`) is who owes us something right now.
- **Pending rates** (`/work/influencers/review`) is every creator nobody can sell yet. If that
  number is growing, sourcing is running ahead of pricing and the sales team has nothing to
  sell.
- **Coverage** (`/work/coverage`) shows clients with nothing moving. A quiet client is a client
  about to leave.

When you ask for something, put a date on it and a number in it. "More influencers" gets you
two. "Seven more in Bahrain by Thursday" gets you seven.

---

## 5. Clients and commercials

- **Clients** (`/work/clients`) is every paying client, with their campaigns and their money.
- **Quotes** (`/work/proposals`) is everything out with clients. Each one shows the total we
   charge, what it costs us, and the margin. You are the only people who see the cost side
   besides talent.
- A quote with a margin that looks wrong usually means a creator's cost is wrong in the
  database, not that the quote is broken. Check the creator.
- Any discount or change to agreed pricing comes to you or Zain. Nobody else gives one.

---

## 6. Rules that do not bend

- **Cost never reaches a client.** The platform hides it. Spreadsheets do not, which is why
  exports are discouraged and why a roster export now asks you to confirm.
- **No money moves without you or Zain.**
- **No voucher or product goes to a creator before the client has confirmed in writing.**
- **A screen that failed to load is not an all clear.** "Nothing is late" and "we could not read
  your queue" are different sentences, and the platform now says which one it means.

---

## 7. Words we use

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| Cost | What we pay the creator. |
| Sell | What we charge the client. |
| Margin | Sell minus cost, as a percentage. |
| Roster | A brand's sourcing list, round by round. |
| Payable | An amount we owe one creator. |
| Payout run | A batch of approved payables, turned into a bank file. |
| Decision | Something waiting on you. Counted in the sidebar. |
| Nudge | Somebody asking you again. Capped at once a day. |

---

## 8. If something is stuck

- You are waiting on the team: say the number and the date.
- The team is waiting on you: it is in Decisions, oldest first. If that list is long, the
  company is stopped and the screen is telling you where.
- A number on a screen looks wrong: say which screen, which number, and what you expected. Do
  not work around it in a spreadsheet, because then two versions of the truth exist.

Inside the platform, **Show me how** (top right) runs a guided walkthrough on the real screen,
**Guide** (`/work/guide`) explains every screen, and **Walkthrough** (`/work/manual`) is the
full story of how work moves, built to be presented to a room.
