# Founder & CEO — your guide

**Who this is for:** Zain. Superadmin. Everything is visible and everything is yours.

**Your one sentence:** you are the only person who can see the whole company at once, so this
guide is about reading it quickly and not becoming the bottleneck in it.

---

## 1. Your day, in order

**First thing**

1. **Today** (`/work/today`). The leadership home screen. The four numbers, what is waiting on
   you, and what is running without you.
2. **Decisions** (`/work/inbox`). Everything waiting on a decision from you, oldest first. If
   this list is long, work has stopped somewhere and this is the screen that says where.
3. **Pipeline** (`/work/pipeline`). Every creator across every brand, next action on each, red
   for late, counts per step down the left.

**Weekly**

- **Targets** (`/work/goals`) against actuals.
- **Coverage** (`/work/coverage`) for clients with nothing moving.
- **Finance** (`/work/money`) and **Payout runs** (`/work/payouts`).
- **Reports** (`/work/report-campaigns`) for anything that concluded.

## 3. Money

**The order things happen in**

1. The account manager confirms a stage is complete.
2. You raise the client invoice. Advance and balance, in the split that was agreed. Our usual
   shape is an advance invoice and a balance invoice, issued together so the client's finance
   team can process both.
3. Business development chases the purchase order if the client needs one before they can pay.
4. Creator payments are prepared by talent in **Payables** (`/work/payables`), approved by you
   or Hajar, and paid in a **payout run** (`/work/payouts`), which produces the bank file.
5. Receipts go back to the creators after payment.

**Guards the platform puts in your way on purpose**

- Above AED 25,000 you type the amount to confirm.
- A payable with no bank details on file cannot enter a run, and the run says so per line.
- A payout run can be read before you act on it. Reading does not create anything; checking a
  run used to be the same action as raising the invoices behind it, and that is now split.

## 4. Pricing, and the thing to watch

- Creator prices live on the creator in the master database (`/work/influencers`), as a cost and
  a sell price per deliverable.
- Quotes freeze the price at the moment they are created. That is correct, because a client who
  received a price should not see it change. It also means a creator's rate card can drift away
  from what we actually negotiated on a recent campaign. When a margin looks wrong, check the
  creator's card against the last real deal before you blame the quote.
- **Our cost is never visible to a brand anywhere in the platform.** Only talent and leadership
  see it. Keep it that way in email too.

## 5. Where your time actually goes, and how to stop that

You are copied on nearly everything. Three habits take most of it back.

1. **Answer decisions from the email.** A money decision or a rate confirmation arrives as an
   email with the amount, the creator, the campaign, and two buttons. You can decide without
   logging in. The link lasts seven days and works once.
2. **Push questions to the screen they belong on.** When somebody asks you something in
   WhatsApp that has a screen, answer with the screen. The second time they ask they will open
   it themselves.
3. **Make somebody own it.** Most things that come to you come because no row has a name on it.
   In Pipeline, tick the creators and use "Give to". A list nobody owns becomes yours by
   default.

## 6. Running the team

- **Staff** (`/work/staff`) is where a staff member's access is set. Default access by role:
  - Talent: creators, quotes, campaigns, the app.
  - Account manager: clients, campaigns, quotes, the app.
  - Business development: clients and quotes.
  - Co-founder and you: everything.
  - You can grant extra modules per person without changing their role.
- **Client visibility.** By default an account manager sees the clients assigned to them. If
  somebody should see all of them, that is set on their staff record.
- When somebody joins, the fastest start is: their role guide, then **Show me how**
  (top right) which walks them through the real screens, then the **Walkthrough**
  (`/work/manual`) presented to them once, out loud.

## 7. What the platform is now honest about

Worth knowing, because the old behaviour trained everybody to distrust screens.

- **A failed read is never shown as good news.** "Nothing is late" and "we could not read your
  queue" are now different sentences everywhere. If a screen could not load, it says so.
- **A number we do not have shows a dash, not a zero.** A zero means measured. A dash means we
  do not know. Margins, reach, engagement and totals all follow this.
- **Every money action asks before it happens**, and names the amount and who it goes to.
- **Nudging is built in.** Anyone can ask you again, once a day per decision, and the person
  asking can see how many times you have already been asked.

---

## 8. Rules that do not bend

- Cost never reaches a client.
- No money moves without you or Hajar.
- No voucher or product goes to a creator before the client confirms in writing.
- A WhatsApp broadcast uses the marketing number only, never the one that sends login codes,
  and an unapproved template fails every single recipient rather than some.
- Nothing goes to a client from a spreadsheet when the platform can send it. The platform knows
  what to hide.

---

## 9. If something is wrong in the platform

Say the screen, what you did, and what you expected. Those three things fix it in one pass.
"It is not working" costs a day.

The guides and walkthroughs live at **Guide** (`/work/guide`), **Show me how** (top right) and
**Walkthrough** (`/work/manual`). They are updated with the product, not after it.
